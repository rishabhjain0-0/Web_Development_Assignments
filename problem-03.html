Indian Railways Completes High-Speed Test Track in Rajasthan

New 70 km Route Set to Revolutionize Domestic Train Speed and Safety Testing

By Tech & Mobility Desk | Published on August 25, 2026 in Jaipur, India

Indian Railways is nearing the final completion stage of its first dedicated high-speed test track, constructed by the Research Design and Standards Organisation (RDSO). Located between Gudha and Thathana Mithri in the Nawa region, the project aims to modernize rail validation processes nationwide.

The state-of-the-art facility was built at a total cost of ₹967.07 crore and spans a comprehensive length of 70 kilometers. Engineers and safety inspectors will utilize this dedicated corridor to test new rolling stock, high-speed locomotives, and next-generation safety systems without disturbing main passenger lines.

During a preliminary trial inspection, senior rail officials highlighted how crucial this infrastructure will be for future mobility projects. "Establishing a dedicated testing environment ensures strict adherence to international safety benchmarks before public deployment," stated a chief engineer on site.
