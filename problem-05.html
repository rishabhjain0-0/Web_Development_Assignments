Science Notes

Chemistry

Chemical Formulas: Common chemical compounds include water (H2O), carbon dioxide (CO2), ozone (O3), and sulfuric acid (H2SO4). Glucose is represented as C6H12O6.

Photosynthesis: The chemical process where green plants convert carbon dioxide and water into glucose and oxygen in the presence of sunlight.

Physics

Kinetic Energy: The kinetic energy of an object is calculated using Ek = ½mv2.

Unit of Volume: Standard unit of volume is cubic meters (m3), while area is measured in square meters (m2).

Speed of Light: In a vacuum, light travels at approximately 3 × 108 m/s.

Biology

Cellular Respiration: Respiration is the metabolic process by which cells break down glucose to release energy stored in ATP molecules.

The primary gas absorbed by plant leaves during daytime is carbon dioxide.

Quick Revision

1. H2O is the molecular formula for water.

2. Gravitational acceleration on Earth is 9.8 m/s2.

3. Chlorophyll is the green pigment responsible for absorbing light energy.
