Student Terms & Rules

General Rules

Students must carry their identification cards at all times while on campus.
Failure to show ID upon request may result in denial of entry to institutional facilities.

All campus property must be treated with care and respect.
Damaging property will result in a fine corresponding to repair costs.

Attendance

Minimum required attendance for each course is strictly enforced.
Falling below the limit will lead to a disqualification from final exams.

Examination

Electronic devices are completely prohibited inside the examination hall.
Any violation will result in an immediate zero mark penalty for the test.

Assignment Submission

All coursework assignments must be turned in through the official portal.
Submissions sent past the deadline will face a 10% daily grade deduction.

Discipline

Unprofessional behavior or harassment of any kind will not be tolerated.
Severe infractions will lead directly to immediate suspension.
